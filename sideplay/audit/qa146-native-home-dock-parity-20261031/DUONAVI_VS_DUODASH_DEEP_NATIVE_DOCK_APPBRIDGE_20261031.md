## Important feasibility/compatibility limits

1. DuoDash version used here is a **REPACK**, not a verified original vendor stock binary. Prior whole-DEB audit [41179261436](https://github.com/chuongnguyen89dn-ui/Duoface/actions/runs/41179261436) confirms 44 package files, 4 Mach-O modules, rootless `com.sensetechlab.duodash 1.1.3+fullscreen1`; its `DuoDashUnifiedFullscreen` was a distinct additional module.
2. Apple's public CarPlay scene entitlement does not make arbitrary unsupported iPhone apps eligible. The DuoFace goal is a **self-developed internal equivalent** CarBridge, not embedding leftyfl1p commercial binary/license.
3. **Actual user's hardware ABI** [run 41186995190](https://github.com/chuongnguyen89dn-ui/Duoface/actions/runs/41186995190): `DBWorkspace initWithOwner:`, `requestStateChange:`, `DBMutableWorkspaceStateChangeRequest activateApplication:` exist, but CarSurf example `DBApplicationController sharedInstance`, `DBWorkspaceState activeBundleIdentifier` not found at logged process/time. These absences may be timing/process, not proof of universal unavailability.
4. Commercial DuoDash official docs [features](https://sensetechlab.com/duodash/features/), [FAQ](https://sensetechlab.com/duodash/faq/) independently describe their public functional claims (non-native app panes, app-specific crashes from tweak conflicts). They do **not** establish that our REPACK binary matches current official behavior, or that changing private APIs by imitation will work.
5. Neither original DEB was run physically in this audit. **No sample measured native Home previous-screen behavior, activation method arguments, app-side scene orientation policy, app Dock object class ownership, or end-to-end first accepted touch.** Do not report a confirmed runtime fix.

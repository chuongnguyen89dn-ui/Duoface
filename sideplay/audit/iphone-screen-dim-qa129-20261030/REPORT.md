**Answer:** Signed current QA129 **does NOT implement Native12's explicit physical screen-blank toggle/handler**, and **does not implement a dedicated dim-lock-screen mode**. It DOES contain a system idle sleep assertion and under-lock **scene** hooks. There is no evidence that DuoFace itself directly invokes `BKSDisplayServicesSetScreenBlanked` to blacken the phone. Native12 having a toggle and BKS symbol proves packaged code references, **NOT its default value, whether it actually runs on phone lock, or whether it maintains a dim backlight**. OmniCar's BKS symbol also does NOT by itself prove it deliberately keeps the iPhone partially illuminated.

## Why this user's observation matters; what is *not* yet proved
When the phone's screen is dim instead of fully off, the iPhone may be in a **different power / foreground / lock-state transition**, which can change remote YouTube video Play behavior despite working scrolling. OmniCar also installs under-lock AppBridge hooks and remote scene behavior; **brightness alone cannot identify which of those caused Play recovery**. The first-party QA129 `DFNativeHostAppDelegate.m` has a strict session owner/lease and can revoke/hide hosted panes on CPTemplate Background if real native owner is not authenticated. `DFTouchOwnerProbe.m` watchdog can detach on stale/invalid lease. These are relevant to the user's loss of apps/Play on lock. No newly uploaded QA129 loaded-UUID + precisely timed physical trace exists; do not claim exact power or touch root cause.

Apple's official Always-On display is a distinct hardware feature; it is NOT universally native to iPhone X and later, and Apple's guidance says genuine Always-On may turn off on CarPlay connection. The dim Lock Screen shown here with OmniCar is a **real user-observed state**, not proof of native AOD; do not describe it as Apple's universal CarPlay default. See `https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/keep-the-iphone-display-on-longer-iph7117338a8/ios`.

Apple's IOPM `PreventUserIdleSystemSleep` only retains **system idle sleep**; it can allow display dim/sleep. Prior audit `sideplay/audit/iphone-backlight-121/LOCKED_PLAY_NEXT_TECHNICAL_DECISION_20261030.md` and Apple `IOPMLib.h` document that distinction; do not conflate the existing QA129 IOPM assertion with a dim-screen or remote video input fix.

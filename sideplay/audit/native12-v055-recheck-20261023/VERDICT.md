## Source-level failure risks, NOT observed physical root causes
1. `DFCarPlayDockOwner.DFZCarPlayProcess` permits `CarPlay`, `CarPlayApp` and `dashboard`; all listeners can independently call `DFZReply` writing the SAME `DFZAck` plist. If one real Dock owner ACKs success and another non-owner ACKs failure, the latter may overwrite or preempt it. Per-process `DFZHandledSession/Sequence` does NOT enforce a single authoritative owner across processes. Real multi-process ACK race is **possible from the source**. Must single-owner arbitrate, then instrument actual ACK.
2. The recreated external `dividerWindow` and `UIButton` are NOT the original `CBDividerView._moveButtons` and `CBDividerButtonInfo.setRelativePos:`/native `updateGripLayout` path that physically passed Native9. Direct touch tracking is NOT proof a finger is delivered by the CP UIWindow on device, survives 3s, and continues as logo moves.
3. `DFCarPlayDockOwner` treats `view.hidden=YES` immediate flag read as positive; visually hidden real Apple Dock, stable scene/window ownership and exact rollback are not demonstrated.
4. Previous YouTube portrait-player/ExitSafeMode complaints remain open until isolated on current installed build. Compilation is not a functional regression test.

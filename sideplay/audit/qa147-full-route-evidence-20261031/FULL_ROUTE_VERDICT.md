## Quantified evidence already on device — not assumptions

- 26 approved Home requests; 26 host-created; **16** `scene-handle-observed-render-unverified`; **0** hosted-outcome with verified PID, **0** native Dock active, **0** ABI refusal; 8 `Scene attach exception: bogus reference size`.
- **All eight failed attaches were followed by a `pending-scene` event with null bundle/request identity.** This corroborates the code's `begin:` continuation after synchronous `sceneFailed:` stops/clears the owner. Source: `LOG23_FAILURE_FINDINGS.json` + `DFHomeStandalone144.m:319-333`.
- Under `DF144Solo.sceneReady:`, positive ACK is single-shot and conditional on `verifiedPIDForHostedView:`. If PID is unavailable at notification time, there is **no retry in this handler**. The 9s watchdog only faults a **non-live** scene. Therefore a live scene with no resolved PID can remain without an ACK. This is a **proven source-state gap**, not yet measured as the actual rejected guard in Log23.
- In 2 of 26 cases the **first host dimensions are transposed** vs requested `595x331.333`: YouTube `07D0259B...` and Facebook `F1ADEC5E...`. YouTube carries `331.333x595` through viewport commit and app reflow ACK despite orientation1. Seven of the eight bogus-reference-size failures have *no* transpose, proving transpose cannot be the sole explanation.
- 15 `scene-frame-readback` logs have phone `375x812` vs CarPlay target `1190x663` (one `663x1190`). They are **precommit snapshots**; no final pixel orientation conclusion.
- Eight ~9.5–11.5s stops are **consistent with** the 10s pending watch, but `native-Home-exit` label is shared with physical Home, so the exact cause of every stop is **unknown**. No claim that the measured pending median is first-frame latency.

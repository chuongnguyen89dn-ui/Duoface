## Source authenticity and exact primary evidence

1. Official SenseTechLab DuoDash features page `https://sensetechlab.com/vi/duodash/features/` documents *Close Apps on CarPlay Disconnect* as a **separate beta switch, default OFF**. When enabled it closes apps running on the car display roughly **12 seconds after disconnect**, to tolerate brief drops; closing apps stops their audio and may lose unsaved work. Distinguish from two other independent options: *Auto Start* on connect and *Close Unloaded Pane Apps* when replacing an app in a pane. Official English page: `https://sensetechlab.com/duodash/features/`. This is developer documentation, not an independent device test.
2. Existing **byte-level original DuoDash 1.1.3 arm64 DEB disassembly** is preserved at `docs/duoface-vs-duodash-autostart-disconnect-audio-20261016.md` and `audit/duodash-1.1.3/settings_license_static_audit.md` in THIS DuoFace repo. Original input file `com.sensetechlab.duodash_1.1.3_iphoneos-arm64.deb` SHA256 `16ffab22b4246dea9978cae92df7ec635d833ec067b4d9f425a231b26edec601`. I read the archived analysis today; **the original proprietary binary was not fetched/redisassembled in this turn**. The archived 2026-10-16 disassembly provides concrete arm64 addresses and is stronger than UI labels alone:
   - Real preference `disconnect_close_enabled` (CFString `0xccb08`, literal `0x80990`), getter at `0x4936c` invokes `CFPreferencesSynchronize`/`CFPreferencesCopyValue` and rejects missing/non-CFBoolean as OFF.
   - Disconnect scheduler `0x491c8` increments a generation counter at `0xdc990`; `dispatch_after` at `0x49344` uses `0x2CB417800` ns = exactly **12 seconds** (instruction region `0x49220..0x49228`). Runtime override file and a kill switch exist in the binary but are experimental and must **not** be copied.
   - Deferred callback `0x4941c` rechecks generation; `0x49448` verifies `carPlayConnected` is still false; `0x49458` re-reads preference; only then calls app cleanup routine `0x43f7c` with reason `carplay.disconnect`.
   - Disconnect detection includes `UIScreenDidDisconnectNotification` (~`0x12cb8`) and `CarPlayIsConnectedDidChange` (~`0x11674`), then `notify_post("com.sensetechlab.appbridge.cpdisconnect")` at `0x115c0`. There is a distinct app inventory walk at `0x48eb4..0x490a0`.
   - **Unknown** from archived byte-level evidence: the final private selector used to terminate each specific app process; whether it protects iPhone foreground apps, Maps audio or Now Playing; how it handles app auto-respawn. NO on-device DuoDash call trace was performed; do not claim it demonstrably always stops YouTube.
3. The `carplaydsp` branch is **NOT the original DuoDash source code**. Its `duodash-fullscreen/DuoDashFullscreen.xm` is an independent **fullscreen adapter**, manipulating dock view and bridge frames; it has no original DuoDash auto-close pipeline. `independent-split/runtime/CPSRuntime.m` has a separate **CarPlay Split** proof-of-concept auto-close after 12 seconds and cancellation by generation; `independent-split/runtime/CPSInterface.inc` uses RBS termination with extra guards. **These are original work of this account, not DuoDash 1.1.3 implementation**. Never confuse their RBS selector guesses with evidence of what DuoDash binary calls.

## Current DuoFace 0.1.105: why the feature is missing

Actual first-party source on current branch:
- `sideplay/duoface-native-scene-host/DFNativeHostAppDelegate.m` stops the **CarPlay foreground control owner** upon resignation/Home/disconnect by revoking the session pulse and notifying `com.duoface.nativehost.touchowner.stop.v1`. Thus **CarPlay Home and a disconnected cable can cause similar chrome teardown**.
- `DFTouchOwnerProbe.m:DFStopProbe` invalidates the session generation, detaches Dock Zoom and calls `[DFNative12Chrome stop]`. Its watchdog considers the CarPlay foreground-owner heartbeat stale after 2.5s. It has a separate `DFConnectedCarDisplay` check based on AVExternalDevice screenIDs and actual CADisplay, but currently uses that to **start** hosting, NOT to schedule app termination after a physical disconnect.
- `DFNative12Chrome.m:stop` immediately hides its own UIWindows, calls `[[DFSceneHost sharedHost] stopAllHosts]`, releases controllers and preserves the versioned `DFSlotWorkspacePolicy.h` **last app choices** for reconnection. `DFSceneHost.m:DFSceneSurface.stop` does UIKit appearance, `setRequestedMode:0`, remote host invalidate, and removes owned scene/window; `stopAllHosts` does not request termination of the underlying iOS **app process**, does not check its audio route and does not have a 10- or 12-second deferred process-close timer. **Therefore closing the CarPlay scene is not the same as closing YouTube's process or stopping its iPhone speaker audio**.
- `DFSceneHost.m:verifiedPIDForHostedView:` can read a PID only when the scene is still live and its SpringBoard application bundle matches; `kill(pid,0)` is **a read-only existence check**, not a kill. Last-persisted `PhysicalBundles` and favorites contain user preferences, not evidence of a live current-session process. Using these to kill would terminate unrelated phone sessions.
- `DFNativeHostRootController.m` has iPhone app allowlist, picker layout, favorites/last restore, voice, language and Dock Monitor; **no verified `Đóng ứng dụng khi ngắt CarPlay` ON/OFF control** in the 0.1.105 build. `Close` in the running-app manager only releases an **owned scene**, not a full iOS process.
- The previous user observation recorded in the Oct 16 report was **YouTube audio kept playing on the iPhone after CarPlay projection to an Android display was disconnected**. No comparison on the actual vehicle CarPlay head unit, PID or audio-route trace has proven a specific root cause. Do not reinterpret this as proof that `DuoDash` or iOS always stops music.

## Precise independent DuoFace implementation plan (NOT executed in this investigation)

A. iPhone `Ứng dụng` / `Kết nối`: one `Đóng ứng dụng khi ngắt CarPlay` switch **default OFF**, independent from `Tự động mở` and from `Khôi phục App khi kết nối lại`. Explain 10s delay, warning about audio/data loss.

B. **Physical disconnect detector distinct from Home**: listen to `UIScreenDidDisconnectNotification` / `CarPlayIsConnectedDidChange` and confirm `DFConnectedCarDisplay()==nil`. A vanished foreground heartbeat alone means CarPlay Home, not necessarily unplugged. At disconnect capture **only bundle IDs and verified live PID belonging to the current DuoFace-hosted session**, before scene teardown. Use a separately-scoped provenance ledger, never favorites/saved layout as termination authority.

C. Schedule **10s grace** with monotonically increasing generation/session token. Reconnect or policy OFF cancels; on callback re-read policy and `DFConnectedCarDisplay` and confirm phone foreground is not that app. Filter out non-owned/non-verified PID, Maps native controller, system apps and unrelated running/media applications. No process-wide `terminateAll`, `killall`, respring or silent music pause.

D. **Actual process-close adapter requires evidence**: verify SpringBoard/RBS iOS16 selector ABI against installed device SDK/runtime or an original proven call trace. Request scoped termination by validated exact bundle/process identity, then check process exit; an accepted request or freed scene is **NOT** proof the app exited. Log `scheduled/cancelled/skipped/rejected/requested/verified-exited/still-running`. Do not claim this feature works before the physical app/PID and audio logs support it.

E. Tests needed: unplug versus CarPlay Home; reconnect at 5s and 11s; double disconnect; switch OFF before timer; unrelated audio playing on iPhone; launched-but-not-visible app; YouTube actual speaker/route; native Maps in CarPlay; same app active on iPhone; unknown PID; rapid stop/start; restart while timer pending. System Dock, .90 YouTube orientation, .92 3-second logo, native Maps and four-app restore must stay unchanged.

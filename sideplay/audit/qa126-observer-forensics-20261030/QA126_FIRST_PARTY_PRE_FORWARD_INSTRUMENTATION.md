# QA126 — targeted SpringBoard observer crash instrumentation (30 October 2026)

## Purpose and evidence
The last confirmed iPhone X iOS16.7.16 crash `main:sideplay/log/Crash-0-SpringBoard-2026-10-30-121458.ips` is a SpringBoard SIGABRT in Apple's `SBAppViewController sceneHandle:didUpdateSettingsWithDiff:previousSettings:` immediately after our `DFManagedSceneObserver` forwards its callback; our `DFUpdateScene` is also in stack.
